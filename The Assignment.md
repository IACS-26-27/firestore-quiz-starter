# The Assignment

Work through the [steps](README.md#steps) first. They get you most of the
way to requirements 1, 2, and 4. The rest is up to you.

## Requirements

1. **Answer the quiz.** A working app that lets people submit their quiz
   answers, and change them later.
2. **See everyone else.** A working display of *other students'* answers.
3. **Make it yours.** A customized look and feel that reflects your own
   aesthetic choices (custom CSS, etc.). See the extras in
   [Step 2](steps/2-show-the-questions.md#extras-make-it-look-like-yours).
4. **Make sense to a user.** A sensible workflow: it's clear when and how to
   sign in, what to do next, and what happened after you clicked something.
5. **Have fun with it.** A *fun* way to display other people's answers. See
   the extras in [Step 5](steps/5-see-everyones-answers.md#extras-show-them-off).
6. **Write your own quiz.** Save your *own* quiz document to the database, so
   people can answer *different* questions. Then give users a way to switch
   between your quiz and the default one. (Start with the last few ideas in
   [Step 6](steps/6-rules-of-the-road.md#if-you-finish).)

## Honors

1. **Do something fun with other people's answers.** For example:
   - A "guess who gave this answer" game.
   - Show users who gave answers similar to yours.
2. **Extend the data model.** Add something new to your own quiz. For
   example, questions could have different types (number vs. text), or each
   question could have a `color` that changes how it's displayed. The rules
   let you add your own fields to a quiz you made, as long as it still has a
   `title`, a list of `questions`, and you as the `author`.
